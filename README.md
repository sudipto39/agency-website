# KINETIX® Growth — Digital Marketing Agency Website

Modern, high-converting **Digital Marketing & Performance Growth Agency** website featuring:

- **Signature Intro / Preloader Animation**: Off-white marquee preloader (`LOADING 0% → 100%`) that expands into a dark hero reveal.
- **Interactive 3D Campaign Globe Hero**: Fibonacci-lattice 3D particle sphere with real-time HUD telemetry callouts and live campaign showreel dock.
- **Full-Funnel Marketing Channels**: Paid Social (Meta/TikTok/LinkedIn), Google PPC & Shopping, Technical & AI SEO (GEO), Performance Video Ads & UGC, CRO, and Email/SMS Lifecycle.
- **Interactive Tools**:
  - Before / After Marketing Funnel Transformation Slider
  - Performance Ad Creative & 3-Second Hook Studio
  - Interactive Paid Media ROAS & Annual Revenue Simulator
  - Live Growth Audit Scope Estimator & Contact Form
- **SEO & Performance**: Semantic HTML5, `MarketingAgency` JSON-LD structured data, Open Graph / Twitter Cards, and `IntersectionObserver`-culled 60 FPS video/canvas animations.

## Tech Stack
- Semantic **HTML5** (`index.html`)
- Modern **CSS3** Custom Properties & Fluid Responsive Grid (`styles.css`)
- Modular **JavaScript (ES6+)** & Mock Data Store (`data.js`, `app.js`)
